## Layout read from the map (to build neighbourhoods from)

- The new bridge and Wurukum Roundabout are about 1.3 km apart along the A3, the road that runs straight south from the bridge to the roundabout.
- The old bridge is about 1 km west of the new one. It carries the Eastern Rail Line, which curves down the east side of the town to Makurdi station.
- At the roundabout the A3 crosses the F121: Iorchia Ayu Road to the west, Makurdi-Gboko Road to the east.
- A small stream runs beside the A3 on its east side and joins the river.
- North-west of the roundabout is dense housing between the railway and the A3, with a cemetery just north of the road.
- The dense grey blocks south and south-east of the roundabout are the market-side neighbourhoods. The exact market boundary is not labelled in OpenStreetMap, so check it on the ground or with photos before building.
