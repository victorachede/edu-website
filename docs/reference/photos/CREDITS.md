## What they show (for building the zones)

- Old bridge (1932, rail and road): a steel truss on concrete piers, low over a very wide brown river, with a service road curving down to it on the near bank.
- New bridge (1978, dual carriageway): a long, flat concrete deck on evenly spaced piers, with no truss above the road.
- Both banks are flat and green. Low roofs, mostly corrugated and tiled, sit on the far bank.
- Wurukum Flyover works in 2026: concrete piers with a wide flat cap and hammerhead top, built next to the roundabout.
